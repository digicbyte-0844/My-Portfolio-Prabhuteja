Project case study

QR-Based Smart Contactless Restaurant Platform

A restaurant ordering and operations platform connecting QR menus, customer orders, kitchen status, table management, and billing workflows.

FocusQR restaurant ordering platform
Project typeWeb application
Case studyArchitecture & workflows

01 / CASE STUDY

Business context

The platform digitizes the restaurant visit from menu discovery through ordering and kitchen fulfilment. Customers scan a QR code to view a menu and place an order without relying on a printed menu.

02 / CASE STUDY

Customer ordering experience

QR-based menus provide a contactless way to browse menu items and submit orders, alongside restaurant table and service workflows.

03 / CASE STUDY

Kitchen and administration

A real-time administrative dashboard surfaces kitchen order status, table management, and billing reconciliation. Firestore listeners support live updates between operational views.

04 / CASE STUDY

Technology and outcome

The implementation uses Angular, TypeScript, Firestore, Cloud Functions, and Tailwind CSS. The project brief reports a 30% decrease in average order turnaround time; add measurement context if publishing this metric.

Engineering notes

Key considerations

  • Keeping customer orders and kitchen status synchronized.
  • Connecting table operations with billing and reconciliation.
  • Designing a low-friction QR flow for diners and staff.
