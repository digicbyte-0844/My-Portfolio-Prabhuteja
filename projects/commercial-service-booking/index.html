Project case study

Commercial Service & Booking Platform

A service-business website combining service discovery, cost estimation, and multi-step booking workflows for customer enquiries.

Focuscommercial service booking platform
Project typeWeb application
Case studyArchitecture & workflows

01 / CASE STUDY

Business context

The platform gives prospective customers a central place to understand available services, estimate costs, and begin a doorstep booking request.

02 / CASE STUDY

Service discovery and estimation

A structured service catalog and interactive cost calculators help visitors explore service options and understand likely costs before submitting an enquiry.

03 / CASE STUDY

Booking journey

Multi-step booking forms collect the information needed to progress a service request, guiding visitors from service discovery to a clear enquiry action.

04 / CASE STUDY

Performance and search foundations

The project brief reports a Vite build deployed with Cloudflare Edge Assets and a 42% reduction in bundle size. It also includes LocalBusiness and FAQPage structured data, an XML sitemap, and Google Search Console work. Retain the 42% figure only if supported by before-and-after build measurements.

Engineering notes

Key considerations

  • Reducing friction between discovering a service and requesting it.
  • Keeping the service catalog and booking flow usable on mobile.
  • Providing search engines with clear local-business and FAQ context.
